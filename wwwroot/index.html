    Select or upload a scene

    Click the 4 corners of the blank art area (top-left → top-right → bottom-right → bottom-left). Drag points to adjust, drag background to pan, scroll to zoom.

    Generate

    Tick the scenes for the still images and, separately, the scenes for the zoom videos. Everything uses the artworks selected in the Artworks tab and is saved in one folder as 0001__scene.jpg / 0001__scene.mp4.

        Print files

        Prepares an artwork for printing: the whole art as one print, or cut into two prints for two frames. Every print gets a 0.5 cm white border, so a 60×90 cm art is a 61×91 cm file. Saved in the artworks folder as 0001_print, or 0001_part1 (left or top) and 0001_part2; the artwork itself is not modified.
